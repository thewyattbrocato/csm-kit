v1 scope

Useful now, intentionally narrow.

csm-kit prepares the evidence for a CSM. It does not make the customer decision or act on your behalf.

In v1
  • Read local account context, CRM notes, product usage, support history, and handoff or QBR inputs.
  • Create renewal, sales handoff, or QBR readouts with source-backed facts.
  • Turn missing evidence into a clear question to resolve.
Not in v1
  • Connect to your systems or send data to a service.
  • Contact customers, update records, or take actions for you.
  • No opaque health score replaces your judgment.

Start with what changed

Read renewal timing, usage, support, and stakeholder signals together before deciding what to ask the customer next.

Evidence coverage sets context

The score reports how much of the required input set was present. It is not a health score; it tells you how much context the readout has.

No proof, no claim

When a fact has no source span, it stays out of the readout. The gap becomes a specific follow-up ask instead.
