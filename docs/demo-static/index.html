Evidence flow

A source row becomes a cited decision.

csm-kit keeps the path visible: local exports enter, exact source spans travel with the claim, and unsupported conclusions stop at the gap.

01input row
02source span
03brief line

Citations are the proof

Every factual line carries a source span such as account.yaml#L7. The span is the exact row a reader can inspect, not a footnote invented after the fact.

Completeness is evidence presence

The score reports how much of the required input set was present. It is not a health score, and it appears before the brief so the reader knows how much trust to place in the rest.

Fail closed, then ask

When a fact has no source span, it does not render. csm-kit keeps the claim out and turns the gap into a specific checklist or handoff ask instead.
